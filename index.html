01 · about

One person who speaks business, builds the data, and checks the AI.

I build the layer between raw warehouse tables and the people (and now the systems) asking questions of them: Snowflake, dbt, Airflow, SQL and Python, with CI-tested models and version-controlled releases.

I came to this from the BI side, where you learn that "revenue" means four different things depending on who's asking. That ambiguity is a business problem before it's a technical one, and it's exactly what breaks AI answers on enterprise data. So I start with the decision someone needs to make, agree the definitions, and only then build.

Today's interesting question isn't whether AI can generate an answer. It's whether the answer is correct, permitted and traceable.
